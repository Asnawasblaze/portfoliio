Place your MP3 files in this directory.

To update the playlist:
1. Copy your music files (e.g., song1.mp3, song2.mp3) into this folder.
2. Open `components/Sidebar.tsx`.
3. Update the `playlist` array with your filenames:

   const playlist = [
     '/music/song1.mp3',
     '/music/song2.mp3',
     // ...
   ];
